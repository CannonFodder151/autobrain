ADR 35 — Exhaust System
========================

Australian Design Rule 35 specifies the exhaust emission requirements
for light vehicles. It covers vehicle exhaust emissions standards
aligned with Euro standards.

## Key Requirements

- Exhaust emissions must meet current Euro standard (Euro 6b for new vehicles)
- Catalytic converters must be maintained in working condition
- Diesel Particulate Filters (DPF) must not be removed or disabled
- OBD-II diagnostic capability must remain functional

## Modification Impacts

Aftermarket exhaust systems are legal provided they:
1. Maintain catalytic converter function
2. Do not increase noise above ADR 51 limits
3. Do not bypass emissions control devices
4. Maintain OBD-II diagnostic capability

De-cat or cat-delete downpipes are illegal for road use in all Australian
states. High-flow catalytic converters are permitted if they maintain
emissions compliance. Resonator and muffler changes are generally legal
provided noise limits (ADR 51) are met.

## Compliance

Exhaust modifications affecting emissions require re-certification.
DPF removal is specifically prohibited under state environmental laws
in addition to ADR 35.
