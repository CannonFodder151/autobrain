ADR 31 — Braking
=================

Australian Design Rule 31 specifies the braking performance requirements
for vehicles. This covers service brakes, secondary brakes, and parking
brakes for both light and heavy vehicles.

## Key Requirements

- Minimum braking performance: 6.43 m/s² for passenger vehicles
- Braking force distribution must be appropriate for vehicle weight
- Anti-lock braking systems (ABS) are mandatory for new passenger vehicles
- Brake fade performance must be demonstrated over repeated stops

## Modification Impacts

Aftermarket brake upgrades (slotted/drilled rotors, big brake kits) must
maintain compliance with ADR 31 stopping distance requirements. Changes
to brake balance (front/rear ratio) may require ABS recalibration.
Brake pad compound changes are generally unrestricted unless they
significantly alter stopping performance characteristics.

## Compliance

Any modifications to the braking system that alter stopping distance,
brake balance, or ABS function must be certified by an approved
engineering signatory before the vehicle can be registered.
