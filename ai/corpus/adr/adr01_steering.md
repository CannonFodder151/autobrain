ADR 01 — Steering
==================

Australian Design Rule 01 is the technical specification for steering
equipment for passenger vehicles. It specifies requirements for the
steering mechanism, steering wheel, and steering column.

## Key Requirements

- Steering mechanisms must be designed to reduce the risk of injury in a crash
- The steering column must collapse in a controlled manner during frontal impact
- Steering wheels must meet minimum impact absorption requirements
- Power steering systems must have a mechanical backup capability

## Modification Impacts

Aftermarket steering wheels must maintain the same diameter range as OEM.
Quick-release steering wheel hubs must be ADR-compliant and registered.
Steering wheel spacers may affect the collapsible column function.

## Compliance

Vehicles must comply with ADR 01 before first registration in Australia.
Modifications to steering systems require engineering certification.
