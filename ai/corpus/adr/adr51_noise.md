ADR 51 — Noise
==============

Australian Design Rule 51 specifies the noise limits for vehicle
exhaust systems and overall vehicle noise. This is the primary
regulation affecting aftermarket exhaust modifications.

## Key Requirements

- Stationary noise limit: 90 dB(A) for petrol, 94 dB(A) for diesel
- Drive-by noise limits per ISO 362-1 testing procedure
- Maximum noise level at any speed: 96 dB(A)
- Horn must produce a continuous tone between 90-115 dB(A)

## Modification Impacts

Exhaust modifications are the most common cause of ADR 51 violations.
Aftermarket exhausts must be ADR-compliant (approved for Australian use).
Non-compliant exhaust systems can result in:
- Defect notice
- Fine (varies by state: $300-$800 typical)
- Mandatory reversion to compliant exhaust
- Potential registration cancellation

Silencer/resonator removal, straight-pipe conversions, and
non-approved catalytic converter replacements commonly breach ADR 51.
Some aftermarket exhausts carry ADR compliance plates.

## Compliance

Exhaust noise is tested at vehicle inspection stations.
Modified vehicles must pass noise testing at annual roadworthy inspections.
Victoria: VicRoads VSI 11; NSW: Roads & Maritime inspection criteria.
