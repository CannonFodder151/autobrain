ADR 43 — Vehicle Configuration and Dimensions
=============================================

Australian Design Rule 43 specifies requirements for vehicle configuration
and dimensions. This covers overall length, width, height, and wheelbase
constraints for different vehicle categories.

## Key Requirements

- Maximum vehicle width: 2.5 metres (excluding mirrors)
- Maximum vehicle height: 4.3 metres
- Minimum ground clearance varies by vehicle category
- Wheelbase and track must remain within manufacturer specifications

## Modification Impacts

Lift kits and suspension modifications may affect overall vehicle height.
Body lifts exceeding 50mm require engineering certification.
Wide body kits must not exceed the 2.5m width limit.
Fender flares may be required to cover tyres exceeding the original
wheel arch coverage.

## Compliance

Any dimensional change that affects vehicle classification or exceeds
the limits in ADR 43 requires re-certification by an approved engineer.
