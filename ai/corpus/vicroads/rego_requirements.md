VicRoads — Registration Requirements for Modified Vehicles
===========================================================

VicRoads requirements for registering modified vehicles in Victoria.

## Registration Process

1. Complete VicRoads vehicle inspection (if required)
2. Obtain VASS approval certificate (if applicable)
3. Submit modification details to VicRoads
4. Pass roadworthy inspection
5. Pay applicable registration fees

## VASS Approval Requirements

A VASS approval is required for:
- Engine swaps or significant engine modifications
- Suspension changes beyond manufacturer's specifications
- Exhaust system modifications (if noise/emissions affected)
- Chassis or body structural modifications
- Steering system changes
- Brake system changes (if beyond pad/rotor replacement)

## Documentation Requirements

For modified vehicle registration:
- Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)
- Build date and original manufacturer
- List of all modifications with part numbers
- VASS approval certificate (if applicable)
- Photographic evidence of modifications
- Engineering calculations (for structural changes)

## Fee Structure

- Standard registration: based on vehicle weight and type
- Modified vehicle surcharge: varies by modification category
- Inspection fee: separate from registration fees
- VASS assessment fee: charged by the VASS signatory

## Penalties

Driving an unregistered modified vehicle:
- On-the-spot fine: $800+
- Demerit points: 4 points
- Vehicle may be impounded
- Insurance may be void

Modification without approval:
- Fine up to $1,600
- Vehicle may be de-registered
- Re-registration requires full inspection
