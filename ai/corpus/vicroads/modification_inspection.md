VicRoads — Modification Inspection Requirements
================================================

VicRoads guidelines for vehicle modification inspections in Victoria.

## Inspection Triggers

A modification inspection is required when:
1. Vehicle has been substantially modified from original specification
2. Engine has been replaced or significantly modified
3. Suspension has been lifted or lowered beyond manufacturer specs
4. Exhaust system has been modified
5. Vehicle has been imported and requires first Victorian registration
6. Defect notice has been issued for modification-related non-compliance

## Inspection Stations

VicRoads authorised modification inspection stations:
- Must be licensed under VASS
- Audited annually by VicRoads
- Must have appropriate testing equipment calibrated
- Maintain records for minimum 7 years

## Common Inspection Items

**Exhaust:**
- Noise level test (VSI 11)
- Catalytic converter presence and function
- Exhaust leaks

**Suspension:**
- Ground clearance measurement
- Suspension travel assessment
- Steering geometry check
- Bump steer measurement

**Engine:**
- Emissions equipment visual inspection
- OBD-II readiness monitors
- Engine identification and compliance plate verification
- Fuel system integrity

**Brakes:**
- Stopping distance calculation
- Brake balance verification
- ABS function test
- Handbrake efficiency test

## Outcome

Pass: VASS certificate issued for registration
Fail: Defect notice issued; must rectify and re-inspect within 30 days
Conditional: Compliance required within specified timeframe
