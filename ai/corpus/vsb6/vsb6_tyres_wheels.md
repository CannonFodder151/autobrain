VSB 6 — Vehicle Safety Standards (Clause 2)
=============================================

Vehicle Standards Bulletin 6 clause 2 covers tyre and wheel
requirements.

## Tyre Requirements

- Minimum tread depth: 1.5mm across entire tread width
- Tyre size must match vehicle manufacturer's specification
- Tyre load rating must meet or exceed original specification
- Speed rating must match or exceed vehicle's top speed
- Tyre pressure must be within vehicle manufacturer's range

## Wheel Modifications

Aftermarket wheels must:
- Fit within the original wheel arch (no protrusion beyond body)
- Maintain manufacturer's bolt pattern (PCD) and offset
- Offset within ±12mm of original specification
- Hub bore must match vehicle's hub or use spigot rings
- Load rating must meet or exceed vehicle's GVM

## Tyre Sizing

Plus-sizing (larger diameter wheel, lower profile tyre):
- Overall rolling diameter must be within ±3% of original
- Speedometer accuracy must remain within ±10%
- Tyre must clear suspension and brake components

## Prohibited Tyres

- Retreaded tyres on steering axle
- Tyres older than 10 years (manufacture date)
- Tyres with visible damage (bulges, cuts, exposed cords)
- Competition slicks on road-registered vehicles
- Non-Australian approved tyres (no DOT/ADR marking)
