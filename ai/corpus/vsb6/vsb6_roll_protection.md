VSB 6 — Vehicle Safety Standards (Clause 3)
=============================================

Vehicle Standards Bulletin 6 clause 3 covers roll-over protection
and structural modifications.

## Roll Cages

Roll cages in road vehicles:
- Must comply with AS 1122 (roll-over protective structures)
- Main hoop material: minimum 4130 chromoly steel (38mm x 2.5mm wall)
- Alternative: mild steel 50mm x 3mm wall minimum
- All welds must be full-penetration or fillet per AS 1554
- Padding required on all bars within head strike zone
- Door bars: minimum one per side, within 300mm of door opening

## Roll Bars

Roll bars (half-cage):
- Must be anchored to chassis at minimum 4 mounting points
- Base plates: minimum 200cm² footprint each side
- Cross bracing required for lateral rigidity
- No modification to original crumple zones allowed

## Chassis Reinforcement

Permitted without VASS (minor):
- Underbody chassis stiffening plates
- Subframe braces (bolt-on, non-invasive)

Require VASS (significant):
- Seam welding for structural rigidity
- Chassis sectioning or repair (must be AS 3992 certified)
- Tube frame or spaceframe construction
- Any modification that alters crumple zones

## Prohibited Structural Modifications

- Removing or weakening factory crumple zones
- Cutting chassis rails without engineering certification
- Replacing structural panels with non-OEM thickness material
- Welding roll cages directly to floor pans without reinforcement
