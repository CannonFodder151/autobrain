VSB 6 — Vehicle Safety Standards (Clause 1)
=============================================

Vehicle Standards Bulletin 6 (VSB 6) provides the national uniform
vehicle safety standards. This clause covers lighting requirements.

## Headlight Requirements

- Minimum two headlights required
- Beam pattern: low beam must not dazzle oncoming traffic
- High beam height: maximum 1300mm from ground
- HID/Xenon retrofit must include proper projector lenses
- LED headlights must be ADR 13/00 compliant
- Colour temperature: white or yellow-white only

## Additional Lighting

Aftermarket lighting must comply with ADR 13:
- Light bars: must be wired to high beam only
- Fog lights: maximum two, mounted below headlight level
- Driving lights: maximum four forward-facing
- Under-body/side lights: prohibited on road vehicles
- Indicators: amber or white-amber only

## Modifications

- HID conversion kits: legal if projector lens is fitted
- LED bulb replacement in halogen housing: illegal if pattern fails ADR 13
- Coloured bulbs (blue, red, green): illegal for forward-facing
- Tinted headlight covers: illegal if light output reduced
- Indicator colour change: illegal (must be amber/white-amber)
