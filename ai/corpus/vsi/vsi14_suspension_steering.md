VSI 14 — Suspension and Steering Modifications
==============================================

Vehicle Standards Instruction 14 covers suspension and steering
modifications and their compliance requirements.

## Suspension Lift/Lower Limits

- Maximum lift: 50mm above manufacturer's design height
- Maximum lowering: 100mm ground clearance minimum
- Combined suspension and body lift: 75mm maximum
- Spring rate changes must not affect handling adversely

## Required Certifications

Any suspension modification outside manufacturer's specifications
requires:
1. VASS approval certificate
2. Suspension geometry assessment (caster, camber, toe)
3. Bump steer analysis for lifts > 25mm
4. Brake hose length verification for lifts > 50mm

## Prohibited Modifications

- Blocks or spacers between spring and axle (unsafe)
- Cutting or heating coil springs
- Removing bump stops or limiting straps
- Modifying steering knuckles without engineering certification

## Steering Modifications

- Quick-release steering wheels: ADR 01 compliant hub required
- Steering wheel diameter: must be within 330-380mm range
- Power steering pump upgrades: must not exceed original pressure
- Steering ratio changes: must be VASS approved
