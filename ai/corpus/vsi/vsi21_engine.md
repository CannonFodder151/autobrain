VSI 21 — Engine Modifications and Swaps
=======================================

Vehicle Standards Instruction 21 covers engine modifications and
engine swaps, including compliance requirements.

## Engine Swaps

Engine must:
- Be from a vehicle of the same or newer ADR compliance year
- Meet emissions standards applicable to the target vehicle's build date
- Retain all original emissions control equipment (catalyst, EGR, PCV, etc.)
- Not increase vehicle weight by more than 5% over front axle

## Engine Modifications

Permitted without VASS (minor):
- Air filter replacement (within original housing)
- ECU tuning (within emissions limits)
- Minor bolt-on modifications (exhaust manifold, intake)

Require VASS (significant):
- Forced induction addition (turbo/supercharger)
- Internal engine modifications (camshaft, pistons, compression)
- Displacement increase (boring/stroking)
- Fuel type conversion (petrol to LPG, diesel to biodiesel)
- Engine management system replacement

## Emissions Compliance

All modified engines must pass:
- ADR 37/ADR 79/ADR 80 emissions testing (as applicable)
- Visual inspection of emissions equipment
- OBD-II readiness monitors complete
- Evaporative emissions system integrity
