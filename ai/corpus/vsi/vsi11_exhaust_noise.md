VSI 11 — Exhaust Noise Testing
===============================

Vehicle Standards Instruction 11 specifies the procedure for exhaust
noise testing at vehicle inspection stations in Victoria.

## Test Procedure

1. Vehicle must be at normal operating temperature
2. Engine RPM set to 75% of maximum governed speed
3. Measurement taken at 0.5m from exhaust outlet at 45° angle
4. Three readings taken; highest value recorded
5. Test repeated at idle for reference

## Pass/Fail Criteria

Petrol vehicles: 90 dB(A) maximum at 75% RPM
Diesel vehicles: 94 dB(A) maximum at 75% RPM

Vehicles with multiple exhaust outlets: each outlet tested individually,
highest reading applies.

## Common Failure Points

- Removed or modified catalytic converters
- Straight-through mufflers without internal baffling
- Exhaust leaks before the muffler
- Non-approved aftermarket exhaust systems
- Turbocharger exhaust housing modifications

## Exemptions

Vehicles manufactured before 1971 are tested at idle only.
Competition vehicles with conditional registration are exempt when
not driven on public roads.
