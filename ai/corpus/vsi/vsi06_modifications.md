VSI 06 — Vehicle Modifications
==============================

Vehicle Standards Instruction 06 provides guidance on the approval
process for vehicle modifications in Victoria.

## Scope

This VSI applies to all modifications that alter the vehicle from its
original manufacturer's specification, including:
- Engine modifications and swaps
- Suspension and steering changes
- Brake system upgrades
- Exhaust system modifications
- Body and chassis alterations
- Electrical system modifications

## Approval Categories

1. **Minor Modifications** (Self-certifiable by owner):
   - Wheels/tyres within manufacturer's specified range
   - Cosmetic changes (paint, decals, interior trim)
   - Aftermarket audio/navigation
   - Tow bar fitting (within rated capacity)

2. **Significant Modifications** (Require VASS approval):
   - Engine change (different displacement/fuel type)
   - Suspension lift > 50mm or lowering < 100mm ground clearance
   - Brake system change (disc conversion, upgraded calipers)
   - Exhaust modification affecting emissions or noise
   - Chassis modification or body lift > 50mm
   - Steering system change (rack, column, wheel)

3. **Major Modifications** (Require full engineering report + VASS):
   - Chassis sectioning or stretching
   - Frame replacement
   - Structural reinforcement (roll cage, seam welding)
   - Conversion to left-hand drive
   - Seating capacity changes

## VASS Assessment

Vehicle Approval Signatory Scheme (VASS) signatories are authorised
engineers who assess modifications against ADRs and Australian Standards.
The VASS certificate is required for registration of modified vehicles.

## Documentation Required

For VASS assessment, provide:
- Complete list of modifications with part numbers
- Engineering drawings/calculations where applicable
- Manufacturer's specifications for aftermarket parts
- Evidence of ADR compliance for critical components
- Vehicle identification details (VIN, build date, etc.)
