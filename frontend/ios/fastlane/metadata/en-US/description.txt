AutoBrain turns your phone into an AI-powered car companion. Track trips, monitor vehicle health, find fuel, log maintenance, and plan routes — all from one dashboard that learns how you drive.

KEY FEATURES
• Trip tracking and history with detailed route maps.
• Real-time vehicle diagnostics and maintenance reminders.
• Petrol price map so you always know where to fill up.
• OBD-II companion support via AutoBrain hardware.
• In-app purchases unlock pro diagnostics and advanced analytics.

DRIVE SMARTER
AutoBrain analyses each trip to surface fuel efficiency, driving patterns, and cost-per-kilometre trends. The AI advisor flags issues before they become expensive repairs.

BUILT FOR ENTHUSIASTS
Log every fill-up, service, and modification. Generate shareable reports for insurance, resale, or just bragging rights.

PRIVACY
Your trips and diagnostics stay on your device unless you choose to share. We do not sell your data.

LEGAL
Subscription auto-renews unless turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage in App Store Settings.
