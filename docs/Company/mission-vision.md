# Mission & Vision

## Mission

Develop, manage, and continuously improve every aspect of AutoBrain — intelligent systems that automate operations, enhance efficiency, and drive sustainable growth.

## Vision

AutoBrain becomes the definitive AI-powered car enthusiast companion: a reliable, privacy-respecting platform that understands vehicles, regulations, and owner needs better than any generic assistant. Every AI feature routes through our 9Router instance for cost control, observability, and model flexibility.

## Core Principles

1. **Deterministic over AI** — Prefer reliable, rule-based paths. Use AI as fallback, not primary logic.
2. **Cost-aware** — Every token counts. Route through 9Router for visibility and control.
3. **Modular architecture** — Separate concerns: backend, AI gateway, frontend, mobile. Fewer containers, clearer boundaries.
4. **Data efficiency** — Vectorise and index data for fast, cheap retrieval.
5. **Documentation as code** — Docs live in Outline (internal) and GitHub mirror (public). Update together.
